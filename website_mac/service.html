For developers, MacBook Neo provides an optimal environment for compiling and testing code. A lightweight chassis and long battery life make MacBook Neo an ideal companion for a fast-paced routine. Script ideas in a coffee shop, edit footage on location, or stream movies with immersive spatial audio and vibrant visual output without hunting for wall outlets.
